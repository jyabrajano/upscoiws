This notice explains how the U.P. System Cash Office collects and handles your personal data in this portal. It is provided under Section 16 of Republic Act No. 10173, the Data Privacy Act of 2012, which gives you the right to be informed before your personal data is processed.

Who is responsible for your data

The University of the Philippines System, through the U.P. System Cash Office, is the Personal Information Controller for this portal.

We do not collect sensitive personal information as defined in Section 3(l) of the Act — no government ID numbers, health information, or similar categories.

Why we process it

  • To confirm you are U.P. personnel entitled to use the portal
  • To show you your own statement of account and no one else's
  • To match disbursement records to the correct payee
  • To keep a record of administrative decisions about accounts

Our legal basis

Processing is carried out under Section 12(c) of the Act, as it is necessary to fulfil the University's obligations to you as personnel, and Section 12(e), as it is necessary for a function of a public authority.

This is not consent-based processing. We ask you to acknowledge that you have read this notice, but we do not ask for your consent to process the data, because the University cannot pay you and cannot show you what you were paid without doing so. Acknowledging the notice is not a permission you are giving, and withdrawing it would not stop the processing.

Who can see your data

  • You — your own profile and your own transactions only.
  • Cash Office administrators — for approving registrations, reviewing changes, and answering queries.
  • Our service providers — the portal runs on Supabase, whose servers are in Singapore, and is served from GitHub Pages. They process data on the University's instructions only.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone else except where the law requires it.

Data stored outside the Philippines

Because our provider's servers are in Singapore, your data is stored outside the country. This is permitted under the Act. The University remains accountable for your data wherever it is held, and requires the provider to protect it to the same standard.

How long we keep it

{{RETENTION PERIOD — to be set with the UDPO, consistent with COA record-retention rules for disbursement records}}

When the period ends, records are securely disposed of or anonymised so that they can no longer identify you.

How we protect it

  • Encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest
  • Passwords stored only as hashes — never in readable form
  • Access rules enforced by the database itself, so the portal cannot show you another person's records even if asked to
  • Every administrative action recorded in an audit log with who did it and when
  • Administrator powers separated: assigned administrators cannot approve their own account changes

If something goes wrong

If a breach occurs that puts your data at risk, we will notify the National Privacy Commission and the people affected within 72 hours of discovering it, as the Act requires.

Changes to this notice

If this notice changes in a material way, we will ask you to review and acknowledge the updated version the next time you sign in.
