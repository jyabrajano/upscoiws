Terms of Use

U.P. System Cash Office Integrated Web System

These terms cover how you may use this portal. They sit alongside the Privacy Notice, which explains how your personal data is handled. Using the portal means these terms apply to you.

1.Who may use this portal

Access is limited to U.P. personnel with a legitimate reason to view their own disbursement records, and to Cash Office staff authorised to administer it. Registering does not grant access — an administrator must approve your account first, and approval may be declined or withdrawn.

2.Your account is yours alone

  • Do not share your password, and do not let anyone else use your account.
  • Do not sign in on behalf of a colleague, even at their request or to help them.
  • Sign out when using a shared or public computer.
  • Tell the Cash Office promptly if you think someone else has used your account.

Actions taken through your account are recorded against you. If you have shared it, you have no way to show that something was not you.

3.What you may and may not do

You may use the portal to view your own records, correct your own details, and download a copy of your own data.

You must not:

  • Attempt to view, obtain or infer another person's records
  • Probe, scan or test the portal's security, or try to bypass its access controls
  • Use automated tools to extract data in bulk
  • Copy, forward or publish records belonging to anyone else
  • Misrepresent your identity or submit account details that are not yours

4.Your own records are still confidential

The portal shows you your own disbursement history, including amounts and the account numbers they were paid into. Treat it as confidential. Screenshots circulated in group chats, forwarded statements, and printouts left on shared printers are the most common ways this kind of information gets out.

5.Bank account information

Your Land Bank account number is stored so the Cash Office can match disbursement records to the right person. The portal does not hold your balance, your deposits, or any other information about the account itself, and it cannot be used to look those up.

Republic Act No. 1405 — Secrecy of Bank Deposits. Your deposits with Land Bank are absolutely confidential under RA 1405, and may not be examined or disclosed except in the narrow circumstances the law allows — your written permission, impeachment proceedings, a court order in cases of bribery or dereliction of duty by a public official, or where the deposit is itself the subject of litigation. That protection binds the bank. The Cash Office is not a banking institution and holds no deposit information, so it has nothing of that kind to disclose.

The Cash Office's own duty of confidentiality over what it does hold comes from Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012) and Republic Act No. 6713, section 7(c) (Code of Conduct and Ethical Standards for Public Officials and Employees), which prohibits public officials and employees from disclosing confidential information acquired in the course of their duties. Unauthorised disclosure may also fall under Article 229 of the Revised Penal Code.

If you obtain another person's account number through this portal — whether by accident, by error, or otherwise — do not record it, act on it, or pass it on. Report it to the Cash Office.

6.Administrators

Administrators can see other users' details in order to approve registrations and review account changes. If you are an administrator:

  • Look at a record only when there is a task that requires it
  • Every action you take is logged with your name, the person affected, and the time
  • You cannot approve your own account changes — ask another administrator
  • Browsing records out of curiosity is a disclosure offence, not a minor lapse

7.Accuracy of what you submit

You are responsible for the details you enter, particularly your account numbers. A wrong number may mean a disbursement cannot be matched to you, or is matched to someone else. Changes are reviewed by the Cash Office before taking effect, but review is not verification against your bank records.

8.Availability

The portal is provided as it is. It may be unavailable for maintenance or for reasons outside the University's control. It is a convenience for viewing records, not the official record itself — {{THE OFFICIAL RECORD REMAINS: to be stated by the Cash Office}} prevails in any discrepancy.

9.Suspension and misuse

Access may be suspended or withdrawn where these terms are breached, where an account appears compromised, or where you no longer need access. Misuse may also be dealt with under {{UP'S ICT ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY — exact title and reference}} and the University's disciplinary rules, and serious cases may carry liability under the Data Privacy Act, RA 6713, or the Revised Penal Code.

10.Changes to these terms

If these terms change in a material way, you will be asked to review and acknowledge the updated version the next time you sign in.

11.Questions

About the portal or your records — upcashoffice@up.edu.ph. About your personal data or your rights under the Data Privacy Act — the Data Protection Officer, listed in the Privacy Notice.
